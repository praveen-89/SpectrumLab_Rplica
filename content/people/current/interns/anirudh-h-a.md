[Anirudh H A](/people/current/interns/anirudh-h-a/) is a Computer Science undergraduate at PES University, Bengaluru, with interests in machine learning, generative AI, and network security. He previously co-founded a startup focused on AI systems for personalized music generation and brand identity through sound. His work explores the intersection of technology and music, including research on hybrid architectures for structured music generation. He is currently working on applying machine learning techniques to the retinal domain, under the Chaksu Project. Alongside his academic pursuits, he has been learning the veena for over ten years and remains deeply engaged in classical music.
