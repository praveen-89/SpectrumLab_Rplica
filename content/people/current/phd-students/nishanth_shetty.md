[Nishanth Shetty](/people/current/phd-students/nishanth_shetty/) received the Bachelor of Technology degree from the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, PES University, Bengaluru India, in 2020. Since 2021, he has been pursuing the Ph.D. degree at Spectrum Lab, Department of Electrical Engineering, IISc. His research interests include generative modelling – diffusion models, flow matching, and generative priors for inverse problems in computational imaging. He is a recipient of the Prime Minister’s Research Fellowship, and the Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship India in 2023 (jointly with Kartheek Reddy) and in 2024. He maintains an open-access repository of his [lectures and teaching resources](https://nishanth-shetty.github.io/teaching/).
