[Ravi Shenoy](/people/alumni/phd-graduates/2015/ravi_shenoy/) is the Co-founder and Chief Technology Officer (CTO) of LightMetrics, a Bengaluru-based deep-tech startup specialized in AI-powered video telematics. Since co-founding the company in 2015, he has been leading the development of innovative computer vision and machine learning solutions for fleet safety and driver monitoring.
