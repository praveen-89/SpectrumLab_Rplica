[Jishnu Sadasivan](/people/alumni/phd-graduates/2018/jishnu_sadasivan/) received the B.Tech. degree in electronics and communications engineering from Cochin University of Science and Technology, Kerala, India, in 2007, the M.Tech. degree in signal processing from the College of Engineering Trivandrum, Thiruvananthapuram, India, in 2009, and the Ph.D. degree from Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, India, in 2018.

He is currently a Senior Researcher with Ericsson Research, Bengaluru. His research interests include signal processing, speech processing, and artificial intelligence.
