[Aniruddha Adiga](/people/alumni/phd-graduates/2018/aniruddha-adiga/) is a Research Assistant Professor at the Biocomplexity Institute at the University of Virginia. His research spans signal processing and machine learning, with a current emphasis on time-series analysis and forecasting. He co-leads the forecasting efforts at the Biocomplexity Institute. His work addresses a broad range of applications, including epidemiology, finance, agriculture, and neuroscience.

[Aniruddha Adiga](/people/alumni/phd-graduates/2018/aniruddha-adiga/) received his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Science in 2018, where his research focused on sparse signal representation and blind deconvolution algorithms. Prior to his current role, he served as a Research Scientist and Postdoctoral Associate at the Biocomplexity Institute, and as a Postdoctoral Fellow at North Carolina State University.
