[Ashutosh Gupta](/people/alumni/mtech-graduates/2016/ashutosh_gupta/) is currently working as a Scientist/Engineer-SF at the Space Applications Centre, ISRO. His core area of research include satellite data processing, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Quantum computing and Blockchain technology.

He obtained his B.E. in computer science and engineering in 2009 from government engineering college, Jabalpur. Subsequently, he pursued his Master’s degree in System Science and Automation from Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru in 2016. He is currently pursuing his PhD in Artificial Intelligence from IIT, Gandhinagar.

Ashutosh joined ISRO in 2009 and has contributed in data processing, classical and AI algorithm development and analysis for several ISRO earth observation as well as planetary missions. Beyond academics, he has keen interest in various art forms.
