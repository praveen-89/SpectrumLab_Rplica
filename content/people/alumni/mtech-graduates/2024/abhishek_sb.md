[Abhishek Shreekant Bhandiwad](/people/current/phd-students/abhishek_sb/) received the Bachelor of Technology degree from the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Birla Institute of Technology and Science (BITS), Pilani, India, in 2020. From 2020 to 2022, he was a Design Verification Engineer at Analog Devices, Bangalore, where he worked on multi-gigahertz transceiver projects. He also interned at NVIDIA, Bangalore, in the HSHUB Architecture team in 2020. He received the Master of Technology degree in Signal Processing from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, India, in 2024. Since 2024, he has been pursuing the Ph.D. degree at the Spectrum Lab. His research interests include optimization, inverse problems in computational imaging, and the application of deep learning in computational imaging.
