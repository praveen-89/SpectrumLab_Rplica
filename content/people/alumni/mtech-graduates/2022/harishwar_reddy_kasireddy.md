[Harishwar Reddy Kasireddy](/people/alumni/mtech-graduates/2022/harishwar_reddy_kasireddy/) (Graduate Student Member, IEEE) received the B.E. degree in electrical and electronics engineering from the University College of Engineering, Osmania University, Hyderabad, India, in 2019, and the M.Tech. degree in artificial intelligence from the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru, India, in 2022.

He is currently pursuing the Ph.D. degree in electrical and computer engineering at UF. His research interests include self-supervised learning, explainable AI, and computational pathology.
