I am a biomedical signal processing professional with experience in developing medical device algorithms from inception. As the first employee at Sattva MedTech, I was solely responsible for the end-to-end design and development of the fetal ECG monitoring algorithm, building the complete signal processing pipeline from the ground up within an early-stage startup. The algorithm subsequently achieved CE certification, demonstrating its technical soundness and clinical readiness.

Currently, I work on cardiac diagnostic solutions, including 12-lead ECG, single-lead ECG, and Holter monitoring systems. My work focuses on developing and validating algorithms for the detection of clinically critical arrhythmias, such as second- and third-degree heart block, atrial fibrillation and flutter, and atrial enlargements.

In addition to algorithm development, I have been actively involved in the Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) regulatory process, contributing to successful CDSCO certification by aligning algorithm design, validation, and documentation with regulatory requirements.

My primary interests lie in applying advanced signal processing techniques to medical and healthcare applications, with an emphasis on clinically reliable, regulatory-compliant solutions.
