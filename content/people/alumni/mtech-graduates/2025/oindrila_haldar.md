[Oindrila Haldar](/people/alumni/mtech-graduates/2025/oindrila_haldar/) completed her B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering from Jadavpur University in 2023. In the same year, she joined the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, to pursue an M.Tech. in Signal Processing, where she was a recipient of the Zeiss-IISc Masters Fellowship. She is currently working as an Engineer in the Camera Systems team at Qualcomm.
