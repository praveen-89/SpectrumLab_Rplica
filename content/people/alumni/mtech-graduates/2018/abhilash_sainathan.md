[Abhilash Sainathan](/people/alumni/mtech-graduates/2018/abhilash_sainathan/) is a Principal Engineer in Technology Development Engineering at Sandisk India Device Design Center, Bengaluru. He holds a Master’s degree in Signal Processing with research focusing on Phase Retrieval algorithms.

His current work spans automation, innovation acceleration and development of engineering practices that enhance memory product reliability and performance. Abhilash has a keen interest in exploring AI/ML adoption for semiconductor data analysis, focusing on leveraging intelligent models to accelerate engineering workflows.
