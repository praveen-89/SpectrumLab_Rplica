The **Spectrum Lab** is a research group led by
[Prof. Chandra Sekhar Seelamantula](https://ee.iisc.ac.in/chandra-sekhar-seelamantula/)
in the [Department of Electrical Engineering](https://ee.iisc.ac.in/)
at the [Indian Institute of Science](https://iisc.ac.in/).
The lab focuses on problems in the intersection of computational imaging and machine learning.
